### Long description (listing)

Aflivra brings Romanian public information into ChatGPT: legislation and court case searches, company profiles, localities and places, weather, transport and train timetables, cinema and cultural events, economic and open-data resources. Search by locality, filters and official reference identifiers. Each supported dataset identifies its source; responses may show cached/stale data when the upstream publisher is unavailable. Aflivra is informational and is not a substitute for official sources or professional legal, tax or financial advice. Public tools work without a user account.

### Starter prompts (3 max)

1. Găsește localitatea Brașov și arată-mi prognoza meteo pentru următoarele 24 de ore.
2. Caută firme din România după numele Dedeman și explică ce informații publice sunt disponibile.
3. Arată cinematografele Cinema City din România și caută programul pentru București la o dată aleasă.

### Positive test cases (exactly 5)

1. **Prompt:** „Caută localități cu numele Brașov.” **Tool:** localities_search(q='Brașov'). **Expected:** valid result envelope with locality matches, city/county context and provenance when available. No authentication; data may be paginated.
2. **Prompt:** „Care e prognoza pentru București (44.4268, 26.1025), 24 ore?” **Tool:** weather_forecast(lat=44.4268,lon=26.1025,hours=24). **Expected:** current conditions and hourly forecast, or explicit upstream failure/staleness, never invented values.
3. **Prompt:** „Ce cinematografe Cinema City sunt în România?” **Tool:** cinema_sites({}). **Expected:** cinema list with ids, cities, locations, addresses and coordinates when available.
4. **Prompt:** „Enumeră poveștile din biblioteca Aflivra.” **Tool:** stories_list(page=0). **Expected:** paginated public-domain story titles and identifiers without sign-in.
5. **Prompt:** „Caută firma Dedeman în registrul de firme.” **Tool:** search_companies(name='Dedeman'). **Expected:** list of publicly identified company matches, CUI only where present, and transparent handling of no matches/unavailable source.

### Negative test cases (exactly 3)

1. **Request:** weather_forecast(lat=999,lon=26). **Expected:** safely rejected as invalid coordinates, not a fabricated weather report or HTTP 500.
2. **Request:** places_search(q='spital',category='neexistenta'). **Expected:** JSON-RPC -32602 error naming the invalid category, without broadening the search silently.
3. **Request:** unauthenticated tools/call(name='refresh_sources',arguments={force:true}). **Expected:** -32602 unknown tool and no background job or source refresh. Operator-only tools are not publicly listed.
